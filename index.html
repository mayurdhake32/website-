The need for legal representation depends on the nature and complexity of the matter. A consultation can help determine the appropriate next step for your specific situation.
What should I bring to my first consultation?
Bring all documents relevant to your matter, including notices, court papers, agreements, orders, complaints, correspondence, and any other supporting documents.
Can I consult in Marathi or Hindi?
Yes. Consultations can be conducted in English, Hindi, and Marathi as per your preference.

Yes, criminal defense and related criminal matters are among our key practice areas. The specific nature of the matter should be discussed during consultation.

Consultation fees are discussed during the initial conversation. Please contact us for more information regarding fees for your specific matter.
